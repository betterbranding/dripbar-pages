FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you want to know before your first visit to The DRIPBaR Edina.

Still have a question?Call or text (612) 429-6822
How do I choose the right wellness service?
Start with your goal: hydration, energy, immunity, recovery, healthy aging, or weight support. Our team helps match services to your goal during check-in, or you can book a Help Me Choose consult first.
What happens during the first appointment?
Your care should be personalized, not one-size-fits-all. During your first visit, our clinical team reviews your health history, talks through your goals, and recommends the therapies that fit you.
How long does an appointment take?
Appointment times vary by treatment. Quickshots typically take 15 minutes or less, while most IV therapy appointments are completed in 30 to 60 minutes.
Is a consultation or screening required?
Yes. Every first-time guest receives a brief medical screening with our team before treatment. It is an important part of delivering safe, personalized care, it is included with your appointment, and it typically takes only a few minutes.
Can I book without becoming a member?
Yes. Everyone is welcome at The DRIPBaR Edina, whether you are visiting for a one-time treatment or looking for ongoing wellness support. Membership is completely optional and is simply our best value for guests who plan to visit regularly.
What should I bring to my appointment?
Just bring yourself. We recommend wearing comfortable clothing, staying well hydrated before your visit, and arriving a few minutes early to relax. We will take care of everything else.
