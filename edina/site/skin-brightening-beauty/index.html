FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you want to know about skin brightening and beauty at The DRIPBaR Edina.

Still have a question?Call or text (612) 429-6822
What does a skin brightening drip actually support?
Our drips deliver antioxidants like glutathione alongside nutrients designed to support a brighter, more even tone and overall glow. They work alongside your regular skincare routine rather than replacing it.
Will this lighten my natural skin tone?
No. These drips are formulated for antioxidant and brightening support, not for changing your natural skin tone. Our team can talk through realistic expectations during your visit.
How often should I come in for glow support?
Many guests build a routine every few weeks to maintain results, often pairing a monthly drip with the IM Glowing quick shot in between. Our team can help you find a cadence that fits your goals.
Is a health screening required before my first visit?
Yes. Every first-time guest completes a brief health screening with our clinical team so we can recommend the right drip and confirm it is a good fit for you.
What is the difference between the drip and the quick shot?
The IV drip delivers a larger volume of antioxidants and nutrients over 30 to 60 minutes for deeper support. The IM Glowing quick shot is a smaller injection that takes under 15 minutes for ongoing maintenance.
Can I pair a beauty drip with Red Light Therapy?
Yes, many guests pair a drip with Red Light Therapy or Infrared Sauna for a more complete glow-focused visit. Ask our team about pairing options when you book.
