FAQ

Questions, answered.

Booking, arriving, and rescheduling at The DRIPBaR Edina.

Still have a question?Call or text (612) 324-4740
What happens after I book?
You will get a confirmation by text and email with your time and our address, 3515 W. 70th St in Edina, next to Original Pancake House. Arrive a few minutes early for your first visit so we can complete your health screening.
Can I walk in without an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome when the schedule allows, and appointments are always preferred so a recliner and a nurse are ready for you. Call or text us at (612) 324-4740 to check same-day availability.
How do I book a consultation instead of a drip?
Choose the Consultations option below. GLP-1 weight management, peptide therapy, High Dose Vitamin C, and Ozone Therapy all begin with a scheduled consultation.
What if I need to reschedule?
Call or text (612) 324-4740 and we will move your appointment. Please give us as much notice as you can so another guest can use the time.
Does the $20 new guest offer apply online?
Yes. The $20 off first appointment offer is applied for first-time guests when you book through this page. One per new guest.
