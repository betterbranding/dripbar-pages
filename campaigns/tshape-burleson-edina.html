Before Saturday, Nov 21

From the Edina call on Monday, Nov 16.

Agreed

  • Launch day is Tuesday, December 1.
  • Pre-launch emails: Sat 11/21 at 1:00 PM and Sat 11/28 at 8:00 AM, using Emails 2 and 4.
  • Prices: $99 for face and neck, $149 for body, plus a free consult.
  • Buttons go to the Edina T-Shape page, so clients see more before booking.
  • The rest of the emails start December 1.

Changes to the Built Emails

  • One email about the body at $149 and one about face and neck at $99. All four now say “starting at only $99.”
  • With Email 4 as the face and neck email, Email 2 covers the body. Its photo is a face, so it needs a body before/after.
  • Email 2 says T-Shape 2 is “now at” Edina. On Nov 21 it should ask clients to book ahead for the December 1 launch.
  • Add the free consult to both. Email 2 says no separate consultation is needed.

Still Open

  • Stacy’s write-up on what to focus on, including December. Not received as of Nov 17.
  • Dates for the December emails.
  • When launch pricing ends. One idea from the call: one week only.
  • Resends for the Saturday emails. 24 hours later would land Sun 11/22 at 1:00 PM and Sun 11/29 at 8:00 AM.
  • A Thanksgiving “turkey neck” angle for the face and neck email (idea from the call).
