Quick Reference

01
Position the Syringe Hold the syringe in your dominant hand like a pencil or dart.
02
Pinch the Skin Gather a mound of skin between your fingers at the injection site.
03
Insert the Needle Insert the syringe at 90 degrees in a quick, snapping motion.
04
Depress the Plunger Push slowly with steady, even pressure until all medication has been injected.
05
Release and Remove Release the skin. Place a cotton ball next to the needle, then remove the needle.
06
Apply Pressure Hold the cotton ball on the injection site for 15 seconds, then apply an adhesive bandage.

Before You Begin

  • Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and water
  • Clean the injection site with an alcohol swab and let it dry completely
  • Rotate injection sites to avoid irritation (upper arms, abdomen, thighs)
  • Store your peptides according to the instructions provided by your clinical team
  • Dispose of used needles in a proper sharps container
