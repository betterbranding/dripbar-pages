The Science, Made Simple

What genetic testing actually tells you.

Your genes influence how you absorb nutrients, produce energy, recover from stress, and respond to food and exercise. A genetic test reads specific markers in your DNA, called SNPs, and translates them into practical guidance you can use.

A Simple Sample

Most panels use a one-minute cheek swab. The NAD Profile uses a quick blood draw. Either way, you're in and out fast, no prep required.

A Clear Report

Your results come back in about a week as a plain-language report. No jargon walls, just what your markers mean and what to do about them.

A Real Plan

Our clinical team reviews your results with you and builds a personalized wellness plan: nutrition, supplementation, IV support, and lifestyle guidance.

Foundation Methylation Panel

For anyone starting personalized wellness. A first look at the pathways behind energy, mood, and cellular repair.

  • Methylation markers, including the widely studied MTHFR C677 and C1298 variants.
  • Folate and B12 metabolism genes such as MTHFR, FOLR1, VDR Taq, SLC19A1, and MTRR.
  • Mitochondrial polymorphism, tied to how your cells produce energy.
  • Vitamin D transport markers.

Methylation touches more than 250 biochemical processes, from neurotransmitter production to immune function. Cheek swab collection.

Know Your Baseline

NAD Profile

For anyone focused on cellular energy and healthy aging. A blood test that measures where your NAD levels actually stand.

  • Measures circulating NAD and its key metabolites.
  • NAD is a coenzyme involved in more than 500 cellular reactions.
  • Covers the processes behind energy production, DNA repair, and cell signaling.
  • Gives you a real baseline instead of guessing.

Quick blood draw at our Austin location. Pairs naturally with NAD+ IV therapy at The DRIPBaR, so you can measure, support, and retest.

Eat For Your Genes

ReGENErate Panel

For weight and metabolic wellness journeys. The most comprehensive look at how your body handles food, nutrients, and timing.

  • Metabolic health genetics, including thyroid function, muscle mass, and inflammation markers.
  • Nutrient metabolism: vitamins D, B-complex, A, E, and C, plus key minerals.
  • Food intolerance risk: gluten, lactose, histamine, caffeine, and alcohol.
  • Taste preferences and eating behavior, from satiety to emotional eating and sleep.
  • Chrononutrition (meal timing) and GLP-1 response markers.

One-minute cheek swab with an optional habits questionnaire. Report in 5 to 7 business days.
