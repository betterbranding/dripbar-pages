Recover | IV Drips and Shots

Glutathione and IV support

Best for: post-workout and recovery weeks

Glutathione is an antioxidant your body makes on its own. Training, stress, and hard weeks can draw it down. We offer it as an IV add-on or a quick shot, alongside hydration and nutrient blends chosen to support how you recover.

  • ✓Antioxidant support for the body after hard training or a demanding week.
  • ✓Hydration and electrolytes to support recovery.
  • ✓Your choice of IV drip or a quick shot, based on your time and goals.
  • ✓A nurse reviews what fits you before anything starts.
Not a treatment for injury or pain. Your Airrosti provider handles your injury evaluation. We support the wellness side.

Measure | VO2 Max Testing

VO2 Max Testing

Best for: getting back to training with a baseline

VO2 Max shows how well your body uses oxygen when you work hard. It is one of the clearest snapshots of aerobic fitness. Many people use it to set training zones and track progress over time.

  • ✓A measured look at your aerobic fitness, not a guess.
  • ✓Personal heart rate zones to train and recover by.
  • ✓A baseline you can retest against as you rebuild.
  • ✓Results explained in plain language.
Wear comfortable workout clothes. Ask us about what to expect when you book.

Measure | RMR Testing

Resting Metabolic Rate

Best for: dialing in fuel and recovery nutrition

Your resting metabolic rate is the energy your body burns at rest. Knowing it helps take the guesswork out of how much to eat, especially when your activity level is changing during recovery.

  • ✓A measured number, not an online estimate.
  • ✓Helps you match nutrition to your current activity.
  • ✓Useful when you are moving less, or ramping back up.
  • ✓A simple, seated test.
Ask when you book about prep and timing.

Lab Panels

Genetic Testing

Best for: when it is the right fit for you

Your genes influence how you recover, use nutrients, and respond to training. Genetic testing is not for everyone, and it is not a first step. If it makes sense for your goals, we will tell you. If not, we will say so.

  • ✓A simple cheek swab for most panels.
  • ✓Insight into recovery, nutrient use, and training response.
