FAQ

Questions, answered.

What Airrosti patients ask us most.

How do I use my Airrosti offer?
Book from this page and mention Airrosti when you check in. That’s it.
Can I come in the same day as my Airrosti appointment?
Yes. Same-day visits after Airrosti are welcome. Let your nurse know about your Airrosti session when you check in.
How long does a visit take?
Most IVs take about 45 minutes. VO2 Max testing takes about 30 minutes and RMR about 20. Lab draws and genetic testing swabs are quick, usually 15 to 20 minutes.
Do you take insurance, HSA, or FSA?
Coverage and HSA or FSA eligibility depend on your plan and the service. Ask our team before your visit and we’ll walk through it with you. You can also pay over time with Cherry.
Can I pay over time?
Yes. Cherry lets you split your visit into smaller monthly payments. Applying takes seconds, there’s no hard credit check, and 0% APR options are available. Payment options through Cherry are subject to eligibility.
Apply with Cherry →
How do I prepare for VO2 Max or RMR testing?
For RMR, skip food, caffeine, and nicotine for about 4 hours, and skip exercise before your test that day. For VO2 Max, eat light 2 to 3 hours before, skip hard training for 24 hours, and wear comfortable workout clothes. We’ll confirm prep when you book.
Do I need an appointment?
Walk-ins are welcome when we have room. Booking ahead holds your spot, especially for VO2 Max and RMR testing.
Is this a treatment for my injury?
No. Your Airrosti provider handles your injury care. Our services are wellness support and testing, and they don’t diagnose or treat injuries or pain.
