Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information The DRIPBaR Burleson collects through this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Effective date: September 18, 2026. Applies to thedripbarburleson.com and the booking, contact, and messaging tools on it.

Who we are

The DRIPBaR Burleson is an independently owned and operated location of The DRIPBaR franchise system, located at 295 E. Renfro St, Ste 207, Burleson, TX 76028. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" refer to The DRIPBaR Burleson. This policy covers information collected through this website. Information collected in person at our location, including your health screening and clinical records, is governed by the notices you receive at your visit.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the website, and feel free to call or text us instead.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Booking requests. When you book online we collect your name, email address, phone number, the service or goal you selected, whether this is your first visit with us, your preferred date and time, and any notes you add.
  • Contact and consultation requests. When you send a message or request a consultation we collect your name, contact details, and the contents of your message.
  • Consent choices. We record when you agree to receive appointment related calls, texts, and emails, including the time you checked the consent box.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, operating system, screen size, and language.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • The pages you view, how you arrived at our site, the links you click, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Identifiers stored in cookies and similar technologies, described under Cookies and analytics.

Health information

This website is not designed to collect protected health information. The online booking tool asks only for your contact details and service selections. Your health screening happens with our clinical team at your visit, not on this website.

If you choose to include health details in a booking note or contact message, we treat that information as confidential and use it only to prepare for your visit. Please avoid sharing detailed medical information through the website and save it for your screening, where it can be reviewed by a licensed clinician.

Our scheduling, messaging, and practice management vendors may store the information you submit on our behalf. We require these vendors to safeguard your information and use it only to provide services to us.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, remind you about, and manage your appointments.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To send appointment related calls, texts, and emails you have agreed to receive.
  • To send occasional promotions or wellness updates if you opt in, which you can stop at any time.
  • To understand how visitors use our website and to improve our pages, content, and booking experience.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising and reach people who may be interested in our services.
  • To protect the security of our website and to comply with legal obligations.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

We use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies through Google Tag Manager to run analytics and advertising tools on this website. These tools may include Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking, and social media advertising pixels. They help us count visits, see which pages are useful, measure whether our ads lead to bookings, and show relevant ads to people who have visited our site.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including deleting existing cookies and blocking new ones. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of the website work. You can also opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings, the Digital Advertising Alliance, and the Network Advertising Initiative. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, visit Google's partner sites page.

Text messaging terms

When you check the consent box in our booking or contact forms, or text us first, you agree to receive text messages from The DRIPBaR Burleson at the mobile number you provide. Messages may include appointment confirmations, reminders, and follow ups, replies to your questions, and, if you have opted in, occasional offers. Message frequency varies based on your appointments and requests.

  • Opting out. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to opt back in.
  • Help. Reply HELP to any message, or call (817) 241-2107, for assistance.
  • Cost. Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Not required. Consent to text messages is not a condition of receiving services from The DRIPBaR Burleson. You can always book by phone.
  • Your consent stays with us. Mobile phone numbers and opt in information collected for text messaging are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these situations:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as scheduling, practice management, messaging, email, payment processing, website hosting, and analytics platforms. They may use your information only to perform services for us.
  • The DRIPBaR franchisor and its affiliates, for brand quality, support, and reporting purposes, consistent with this policy.
  • Legal requirements, when we believe disclosure is required by law, regulation, or legal process, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our guests, our team, or others.
  • Business transfers, if our location is sold or merged, in which case your information may transfer to the new owner under the same protections.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (https) for this website and access controls that limit information to team members who need it to do their jobs. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We keep website information for as long as needed to provide services, meet legal and recordkeeping obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Appointment records are kept in line with our clinical recordkeeping requirements.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may ask us to stop marketing communications at any time. Texas residents may have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, including the right to confirm whether we process your data and to opt out of targeted advertising. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and some information may be retained where the law requires.

Email marketing includes an unsubscribe link in every message. Text messaging can be stopped by replying STOP. Cookie and advertising choices are described under Cookies and analytics.

Children

Our services are intended for adults, and this website is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information through this website, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

This website links to third party sites, including Google Maps and The DRIPBaR corporate site. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of other sites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version here and update the effective date at the top of the page. Continued use of the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.
