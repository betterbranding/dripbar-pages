## 1. Edina at a Glance

Edina is one of the wealthiest, most highly educated suburbs in the Twin Cities metro. It sits just southwest of Minneapolis (the Southdale area is the cultural and commercial heart) and is known for top-tier schools, an active outdoor lifestyle, and a strong wellness-and-fitness culture that already supports premium operators like Life Time, Orangetheory, and a growing roster of boutique studios and med spas.

| Average household income | $214,711 | — | — |
| Bachelor's degree or higher | ~72% | ~42% | ~36% |

The household income is roughly **1.5× the metro average and 1.6× the U.S. median.** Average household income (which captures the top end) is more than $214K — meaning a meaningful cohort of dual-income executive, medical, and legal professionals.

## 2. Primary Target Demographic

### Core Persona — "The Engaged Professional"
- **Age:** 38–58
- **Gender skew:** 60% female / 40% male
- **Household income:** $150K+
- **Profile:** Married professionals or executives, often dual-income. Many are physicians, attorneys, finance professionals, or business owners. They have kids in Edina Public Schools (one of the highest-rated districts in Minnesota) and prioritize their own longevity so they can keep up with family, career, and an active lifestyle.
- **Mindset:** Educated consumers. They research before they spend. They've already tried fitness, supplements, and a primary care relationship — they're now looking for the next tier of personalized, science-backed wellness.

### Secondary Persona — "The Longevity Seeker"
- **Age:** 55–70
- **Profile:** Empty-nesters and pre-retirees. Strong disposable income, often active in golf (Edina Country Club), tennis, pickleball, cycling, and travel. Focused on healthspan, joint recovery, energy, and cognitive vitality.
- **Mindset:** They've watched parents age and want a different outcome. Receptive to NAD+, peptide therapy, and immune support framed around resilience and aging well.

### Tertiary Persona — "The Performance Mom"
- **Age:** 32–45
- **Profile:** Active moms juggling careers, kids' sports, and a personal wellness routine. Members at Life Time or boutique studios. Already use IV hydration recreationally.
- **Mindset:** Time-poor, value-focused. They want efficient interventions: a 45-minute drip on lunch break is more realistic than a multi-step regimen.

## 4. Cultural & Behavioral Insights

- **Minnesota winters drive immune & SAD-adjacent demand.** Vitamin D, NAD+, immune drips, and energy support are seasonal must-haves Oct–March.
- **Lake-and-cabin summer culture (June–Aug)** creates a hydration and recovery use case. Many Edina residents own or rent cabins in northern MN and travel weekends.
- **"Minnesota Nice" tone** — direct boasting underperforms. Reserved confidence wins. Educational framing wins. Social proof wins.
- **High health literacy.** Audience tolerates (and rewards) clinical language. They want to know the science.
- **Lutheran/Scandinavian cultural baseline** — practical, understated, family-oriented. Avoid hype, glitz, and influencer-style copy.
- **Wellness IS social currency** in Edina the way golf or boating is. Members of Life Time, Edina Country Club, and Indian Hills overlap heavily.

## 6. Brand Voice — Edina (Mirroring Keller)

Edina adopts the **Keller voice baseline** with Minnesota cultural adjustments. The result is a voice that feels professional, calm, and credible — never loud, never aspirational-for-the-sake-of-it.

### Voice Pillars
1. **Professional, approachable.** Talk like a knowledgeable friend who happens to be in medicine. Not a salesperson. Not a hype account.
2. **Confident without being loud.** State the science. Let it stand on its own. No exclamation points. No superlatives.
3. **Education-driven.** Every post teaches something. Lead with the "why it works" before the "what we offer."
4. **Calm authority.** No urgency manipulation. No fake scarcity. We're the grown-ups in the wellness category.
5. **"Raising the BaR" theme.** Used to signal advancement, science, and the next tier of wellness — same as Keller.

### Edina-Specific Adjustments
- **Reserved confidence.** Even softer than Keller on superlatives. Minnesota audiences read boasting as untrustworthy.
- **Seasonal anchoring.** Weather, daylight, and outdoor seasons matter here in a way they don't in Texas. Reference them.
- **Local geography sparingly but specifically.** Southdale, 50th & France, France Ave, Lake Cornelia, Edina Country Club area, Centennial Lakes. Don't overdo it.
- **No "y'all," no Texas idioms.** Stay neutral-Midwestern.
- **Skip "fixin' to," "howdy," and any folksy register.** Edina is sophisticated suburban.

### Words & Phrases to Use
- "Backed by science," "clinically administered," "under medical direction"
- "Targeted support," "cellular-level," "delivered directly to your bloodstream"
- "Raising the bar on what's possible," "the next tier of wellness"
- "Whether you're recovering from a long week or training for your next event"

### Words & Phrases to Avoid
- "Game-changer," "miracle," "magic," "instant fix"
- "Crush your day," "level up" (too gym-bro)
- All emojis
- Newspaper-style capitalized headlines
- "Y'all," "howdy," any Texas-isms
- "Cure," "treat," "diagnose," any banned medical claims (see core compliance rules)
