04 — Culture

Cultural & behavioral insights

The "Minnesota Nice" cultural baseline shapes how Edina shops, listens, and buys. Loud doesn't translate. Substance does.

Winter drives immune demand

Minnesota winters create real seasonal demand. Vitamin D, NAD+, immune drips, and energy support are essentials from October through March.

Lake-and-cabin summer

June through August, Edina residents head to northern Minnesota cabins on weekends. Hydration and recovery becomes the summer use case.

"Minnesota Nice" tone

Direct boasting underperforms. Reserved confidence wins. Educational framing wins. Social proof wins. Hype reads as suspicious.

High health literacy

The audience tolerates — and rewards — clinical language. They want to understand the science before they spend.

Understated affluence

Lutheran/Scandinavian cultural baseline. Practical, family-oriented, allergic to glitz. Avoid influencer-style copy.

Wellness as social currency

Wellness functions the way golf or boating does in Edina. Members of Life Time, Edina Country Club, and Indian Hills overlap heavily.

05 — Voice

Brand voice — Edina

Edina adopts the Keller voice baseline with Minnesota cultural adjustments. The result is a voice that feels professional, calm, and credible — never loud.

Voice Pillars

Five principles that define how we sound.

01

Professional, approachable

Knowledgeable friend in medicine. Not a salesperson. Not a hype account.

02

Confident, not loud

State the science. Let it stand. No exclamation points. No superlatives.

03

Education-driven

Every post teaches something. Lead with why it works before what we offer.

04

Calm authority

No urgency manipulation. No fake scarcity. We're the grown-ups in the category.

05

Raising the BaR

Used to signal the next tier of wellness — same as Keller.

+ Use

  • "Backed by science"
  • "Clinically administered"
  • "Under medical direction"
  • "Targeted support"
  • "Cellular-level"
  • "Delivered directly to your bloodstream"
  • "Raising the bar on what's possible"

− Avoid

  • "Game-changer," "miracle," "magic"
  • "Crush your day," "level up"
  • All emojis
  • Newspaper-style capitalized headlines
  • "Y'all," "howdy," any Texas-isms
  • "Cure," "treat," "diagnose"
  • Any banned medical claims
