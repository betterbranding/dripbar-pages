## 6. Brand Voice and Writing Rules

### 6.1 Core Writing Style
- Simple, direct, short sentences
- Active voice
- Second person (you/your)
- No emojis
- No em dashes; use commas, periods, or semicolons
- No hashtags, markdown, or asterisks in copy body
- No filler phrases or loose connectors
- Write the way a human would speak
- Start sentences with "and" or "but" when natural
- State facts plainly; steer clear of hype

### 6.2 Caption Rules
- All captions under 2,000 characters (including hashtags)
- Do NOT open with a capitalized headline or newspaper-style title
- Open with a realistic, conversational hook that stops the scroll
- Medical director attribution required before hashtags in every caption

### 6.3 CAFE Framework (never mention by name)
- **Captivate:** Hook intro, attention grabber
- **Agitate:** Provoke emotion of the pain/struggle. Relate with the reader.
- **Fascinate:** Something outrageous they didn't know.
- **Educate:** Follow up with science and evidence-based education. Simplified.

### 6.4 EEAT Framework (never mention by name)
- **Experience:** Real client care, real visits, real questions answered
- **Expertise:** Trained clinical team, licensed medical oversight
- **Authoritativeness:** Professional guidance, individualized support, data-backed protocols
- **Trust:** Safety, comfort, clear explanations, no-pressure environment

### 6.5 Banned Words
Elevate, Delve, Hustle and bustle, Revolutionize, Foster, Realm, Remnant, Subsequently, Nestled, Enigma, Whispering, Sights unseen, Sounds unheard, A testament to, Dance, Metamorphosis, Indelible, Leverage, Synergy, Scalable, Optimize, Empower, Innovative, Disruptive, Robust, Seamless, Holistic, Cutting-edge, Next-generation, User-centric, Agile, Dynamic, Frictionless, Value-driven, Data-driven, Scalability, Mission-critical, Thought leadership, Turnkey, Paradigm shift, Game-changer, Ecosystem, Deep dive, Move the needle, Circle back, Actionable insights, Ideation, Pain point, Deliverables, Quick win, Elevator pitch, Framework, Drill down, Synergize

## 7. Channel-Specific Rules

### 7.1 Instagram + Facebook
- Create ONE post targeting both IG and FB simultaneously
- Use IG caption style (longer form)
- Include 10-15 relevant hashtags at the end
- Do NOT include a URL or booking link
- No emojis

### 7.2 Google Business Profile (GBP)
- No hashtags
- The ONLY channel that uses a URL call to action
- Always include a contextual link to the relevant service page from the location sitemap
- Include gmbPostDetails with gmbEventType STANDARD

### 7.3 Email
- Frequency: 1/week
- Preferred days: Tuesday, Thursday (campaigns on Friday)
- Default status: DRAFT
- BCC greg@thebetterbranding.com on every email
- Preview campaign emails to Greg before sending
- Standard booking CTA in emails

### 7.4 Other Channels
- Pinterest: 1/week
- X: 1/week
- TikTok: 1/week

### 7.5 Posting Cadence
- Instagram/Facebook: 3-4/week
- GBP: 2-3/week
- All others: 1/week

## 8. Compliance

### 8.1 Meta Medical Claims (CRITICAL)
- Never make claims about therapies curing, treating, or preventing any condition
- Use wellness language only
- Focus on how people feel, self-care, and general well-being
- No specific medical outcomes

### 8.2 Medical Director Attribution
- Required in every caption before hashtags
- CV/FM locations: "Under the medical direction of Dr. Angela Straface MD"
- All other locations: "Under the medical direction of Dr. Aminidhan D. Thakkar MD"

### 8.3 Facility Language
- Refer to locations as "locations" or "clinics," not "facilities"
- Use language consistent with a professional wellness lounge

### 8.4 Keller-Specific
- Do not use the word "peptides" in consumer-facing copy
- Use "medical protocols" or "functional support" instead

### 8.5 CV/FM-Specific (Colleyville)
- No esthetics services content (no Botox, Dysport, HydraFacial)
- Flower Mound does offer esthetics (Xeomin, Botox, Dysport)
