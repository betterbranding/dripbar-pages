Questions?

FAQs

What is a Licensed Nurse Practitioner?
↓
A Functional NP looks beyond symptoms to find root causes. They combine conventional medical training with a holistic approach to build protocols tailored to your unique biology. Think of it as healthcare that treats you as a whole person, not a checklist.
Do I need to do all three tests?
↓
No. Each service stands on its own. Your Functional NP will recommend the right combination based on your goals, health history, and consultation tier. Some clients start with labs and add genetic testing later. Your protocol, your pace.
How long until I see results?
↓
Lab results typically return within 5-7 business days. Genetic testing takes 2-3 weeks. VO2 max results are available immediately. Most clients notice meaningful changes in energy, sleep, and focus within 4-6 weeks of following their personalized protocol.
Is this covered by insurance?
↓
Consultations and wellness services are paid out-of-pocket. We provide superbills that you can submit to your insurance for potential reimbursement. Many HSA and FSA accounts cover these services. Investing in prevention is always more affordable than treating disease.
What makes this different from a regular doctor visit?
↓
Depth. Time. Personalization. A standard visit runs 10 minutes and checks a handful of markers. We test 100+ biomarkers, read your genetic code, measure your fitness ceiling, and build a protocol that's yours alone. This is healthcare designed for people who refuse to settle for average.
