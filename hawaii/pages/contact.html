Frequently Asked Questions

How does the telehealth process work? ▼

After placing your order, you'll complete a health questionnaire and schedule a video consultation with a licensed physician. If approved, your prescription is sent to our partner pharmacy for fulfillment and shipped to your door.

Is my information secure? ▼

Absolutely. All data is encrypted and handled in compliance with HIPAA regulations. Your medical information is only shared with the treating physician and pharmacy.

Do you ship to all Hawaiian islands? ▼

Yes! We deliver to all major Hawaiian islands including Oahu, Maui, Big Island, Kauai, Molokai, and Lanai.

What if I'm not approved? ▼

If the physician determines that the requested medication isn't right for you, you'll receive a full refund. Your health and safety always come first.
